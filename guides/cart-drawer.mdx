# Cart drawer

LuminOS includes an enhanced **cart drawer** (overlay) with optional countdown, recommendations, free shipping progress, gift wrap, and mix & match bundle headers.

## Setup

1. Open **Customize** and select the cart drawer / overlay group (not only the cart page).
2. Enable or arrange blocks:
   - Heading
   - Products list
   - Summary / checkout
   - Free shipping bar (tiered goals)
   - Countdown
   - Recommendations
3. Under **Theme settings → Cart**, set notes, discount field, installments, gift wrap mode, and bundle header colors.
4. Add items in preview and confirm drawer open, shipping bar thresholds, and checkout button.

## Free shipping bar

Configure goal amounts to match your shipping rates. Multiple tiers show progress toward the next reward.

## Gift wrap

Follow theme settings for nested vs list gift-wrap product modes. The gift wrap product must exist and be available.
