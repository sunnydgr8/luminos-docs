# Lumin Kit reviews

LuminOS supports two review paths:

| Path | Section | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meta Reviews** | Lumin Meta Reviews | Shopify metaobjects only — [guide](/guides/meta-reviews) |
| **Lumin Kit reviews** | Lumin Reviews / Kit section | **Lumin Kit** app + app proxy |

Use **Kit** when you want sync, moderation, or collection workflows in the app. Use **Meta Reviews** when you want everything inside Shopify admin/metaobjects with no Kit app.

## Setup (Kit path)

1. Install **Lumin Kit** and complete its onboarding.
2. Confirm the app proxy / storefront route the theme section expects is active.
3. In the theme editor, open a product template.
4. Add the **Lumin Reviews** (Kit) section — not Meta Reviews.
5. Sync or import reviews in the app, then preview the product page.

## Do not mix on one template

Avoid stacking both Meta Reviews and Kit review sections on the same product template unless you intentionally want two widgets. Pick one source of truth.

## Still stuck?

Ask in [Setup help](https://community.lumintheme.com/c/setup-help) and specify Meta vs Kit.
