# Lumin Discount App

The theme’s mix & match UI shows tier messaging in the storefront. **Checkout discounts** are applied by the **Lumin Discount App**. Without it, customers may see “X% off” in the builder while paying full price.

## When you need it

- You use [Mix and match bundles](/guides/mix-and-match-bundles)
- You need automatic percentage (or tier) discounts based on bundle cart properties

## Setup checklist

1. Install **Lumin Discount App** from your Lumintheme distribution channel.
2. Open the app and enable discounts for LuminOS mix & match / `_lumin_bundle_*` line properties.
3. Align tier percents in the app with the percentages shown in the Bundle Builder section.
4. Place a test order (development store) and confirm the discount line appears at checkout.
