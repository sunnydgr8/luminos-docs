# Quantity breaks

Quantity breaks let shoppers pick multi-unit deals on the product page. LuminOS also supports **unlock** rows that reveal extra products when a tier is selected.

## Setup

1. Customize a **product** template.
2. Inside the product details / information area, **Add block → Quantity breaks**.
3. Add **Quantity break option** blocks for each tier (quantity, label, compare/sale presentation).
4. Optionally add **unlock** blocks for bonus products tied to a tier.
5. Align pricing with Shopify variants or your discount strategy so the UI matches checkout reality.

## Tips

- Keep 2–4 tiers for clarity.
- If you also use mix & match bundles, do not put both complex offer UIs on the same PDP without a clear primary CTA.
- Theme settings for buttons and prices affect how tiers look.
