# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — lumin-lander-1

Initial LuminOS documentation site launch aligned with theme version **1.0.0 — lumin-lander-1** (author: Lumintheme).

### Theme highlights in this line

- Mix & match bundle builder (with Lumin Discount App for checkout)
- Lumin Meta Reviews (metaobjects) and Lumin Kit reviews section
- Quantity breaks and unlock products
- Frequently bought together
- Mega menu (tabs, products, images)
- Enhanced cart drawer (free shipping bar, recommendations, gift wrap)
- Marketing sections: FAQ, banner grid, pricing tiers, changelog, email popup, and more

### Docs

- Mintlify help center at `docs.lumintheme.com`
- Community forum at `community.lumintheme.com`
- Section reference pages generated from theme Liquid schemas (`npm run generate`)
